Aftercare Guide

Protect your investment and maintain that flawless, freshly-applied look for weeks to come.

THE PHILOSOPHY

The Secret to Lasting Beauty

Beautiful lashes and brows are a partnership. We provide the meticulous artistry in the studio, and you provide the essential care at home. Following these simple guidelines ensures your extensions remain healthy, full, and beautifully styled.

The Crucial First 24 Hours

The adhesive needs time to fully cure. During this window, you must be extra careful.

Avoid Water

Keep your lashes absolutely dry. No swimming, washing your face with splashing water, or crying.

No Heat or Steam

Avoid saunas, steam rooms, hot yoga, and standing directly in front of a hot oven.

Don't Touch

Do not rub your eyes, pull on your extensions, or sleep face-down into your pillow.

The Daily Routine

01

Cleanse Daily

Use a designated, oil-free lash cleanser and a soft brush to gently remove makeup, dead skin cells, and oils from the lash line. Rinse thoroughly with cool water.

02

Brush Gently

Use a clean mascara wand (spoolie) to brush your lashes daily. Support the lashes from underneath and gently twirl the brush to separate them. Never brush while wet.

03

Sleep Smart

Sleep on your back if possible. Using a silk or satin pillowcase reduces friction, preventing premature shedding and tangling of your extensions.

The Do's

  • Wash your lashes daily with a lash-safe foaming cleanser.
  • Brush them gently when they are completely dry.
  • Pre-book your fills every 2-3 weeks to maintain a full look.
  • Use oil-free makeup removers around the eye area.

The Don'ts

  • Do NOT use mascara on volume or hybrid extensions.
  • Do NOT pick, pull, or rub your extensions.
  • Do NOT use oil-based skincare, makeup, or cleansers near the eyes.
  • Do NOT use an eyelash curler on your extensions.
