YOUR JOURNEY

The Consultation

Every great set begins with a conversation. We analyze your eye shape, natural lash health, and daily lifestyle to determine the perfect weight, length, and curl for your extensions.

CUSTOM DESIGNS

Signature Styles

Each set is completely customized to your eye shape. Here are three of our most requested foundational maps.

Cat Eye

Elongates the eye by placing the longest lashes at the outer corners. Perfect for almond or round eyes seeking a sultry, winged effect.

Doll Eye

Opens up the eye with the longest lashes concentrated in the center. Ideal for hooded or monolid eyes to create a bright, awake look.

Squirrel

A specialized mapping that balances the arch of the brow to lift drooping outer corners. The longest lashes hit just before the outer corner.
